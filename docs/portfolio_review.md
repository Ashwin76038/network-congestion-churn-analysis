2. 1,135 assigned/logged label conflicts (88.67%) demonstrate the danger of joining separately generated domains as if they were verified infrastructure.
3. Ten simulation IDs and 12 source-address groups have different scopes. The 9.83% daily-average scenario ratio cannot establish real peak congestion.
